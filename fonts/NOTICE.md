# Font attribution

`Cunning-Poppins-subset.woff2` is a subset of **Poppins** (weight 800 / ExtraBold),
containing only the glyphs used on the card faces (`C U N I G` and digits `0-9`)
to keep the card SVGs small and self-contained.

- Original font: Poppins, by Indian Type Foundry — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Poppins
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (free and open source)
- Subsetted with [fonttools](https://github.com/fonttools/fonttools) (`pyftsubset`)

See the SIL OFL 1.1 text at https://openfontlicense.org/ for full license terms.
